# Duckbill

> Duckbill is an AI-plus-human task-completion service that executes real-world errands — fighting refunds and billing disputes, cancelling services, getting vendor quotes, and booking appointments and reservations — by pairing AI planning with background-checked human "doers". It exposes this execution layer to AI agents through a hosted MCP (Model Context Protocol) server ("Connect to Claude"), letting a developer or agent hand off any task that needs real human follow-through. Backed by Forerunner Ventures, General Catalyst, and Uncork Capital.
